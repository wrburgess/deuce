# Authoring rules

Standing guidance for how the AC writes anything the HC reads. This file is what Chapter 0 means by
"the AC's authoring rules": the judgment half of the writing contracts, the half no automated check
can decide ([Chapter 0](../sds/00-identity-and-governance.md) → *Work Tracking System* →
*Enforcement*).

- **Scope: every artifact written for the HC to read** — an issue's Summary (HC), the HC half of
  every Readout, a stop's question, and the Delivery Record's prose fields. Fixed at the Direction
  gate on #12.
- **Shape is not governed here.** Decision-facing writing takes its shape from the Readout
  ([Chapter 1](../sds/01-lifecycle-and-skills.md) → *The Readout*). This file governs what shape
  cannot: the words, and the depth.
- **Growth bar:** a line enters this file only with cited evidence of the recurring defect it
  prevents ([Chapter 1](../sds/01-lifecycle-and-skills.md) → *Rules*). The two rules below carry the
  receipts that admitted them.

## The register rule

- **The rule:** write in plain words. Prefer the everyday word to the term of art; where a canon
  term is needed, Chapter 0's gloss rule applies
  ([Chapter 0](../sds/00-identity-and-governance.md) → *Vocabulary*) — and a gloss is not licence
  to continue in jargon after it.
- **The test:** could the HC judge from this alone — no term to translate, no need to read the
  technical half?
- **The receipt:** [ace #163](https://github.com/wrburgess/ace/issues/163). The HC rejected
  ordinary agent prose as "not efficient for HC screen reading," then rejected a controlled-language
  rewrite of the same passage as "overly stilted and inefficient." The target register sits between
  those two failures: plain, direct, and dense — simple words carrying full content, not simplified
  content.

## The altitude rule

- **The rule:** match the depth of an explanation to what the reader must decide. Lead with the
  practical impact; go into mechanism only where the decision needs it, or when asked.
- **The test:** does the explanation start at what the reader must decide, not at how the thing
  works?
- **The receipt:** [ace #132](https://github.com/wrburgess/ace/issues/132). The recurring
  frictions behind the rule: technical framing the reader did not ask for, re-asking questions
  already answered, and explanations that opened with mechanism instead of impact.
