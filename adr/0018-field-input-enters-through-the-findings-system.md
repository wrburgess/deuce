# ADR 0018: Field input enters through the Findings System

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-08-04

## Decision

**Field input** — outside material on any of the four channels — enters deuce through the
**Findings System**, not through an intake pipeline of its own. A piece of outside material worth
anything is recorded as a finding at the moment of reading, carrying its provenance; from that
moment it is indistinguishable in handling from anything a run noticed itself. It drains through
the triage pass, and promotion to tracked work is triage's call.

**The predecessor's four intake Skills — `scout`, `clip`, `follow`, `restock` — collapse into that
door, and none ports.** Capture is the Findings System's recording rule, which is a sentence, not
a procedure; the refresher's job is the hygiene sweep's re-verification direction.

## Why (the trade-off that was live)

The intake already exists. Chapter 2 built recording-as-delivery, the two axes, the three states,
the registers, and the pass that drains them. A parallel intake pipeline would be a second copy of
every one of those pieces, and the two copies would drift the way all second copies do — the same
argument that keeps a value out of two files, applied to machinery.

The boundary a second system would need does not survive contact. A platform stall observed in a
session is unsolicited discovery; the vendor note explaining it is field input. They are one
lesson, and routing them into two systems splits one fact across two homes. Chapter 2 already
ruled the routing question once: unsolicited discovery is routed, never capped, wherever it comes
from.

The predecessor's evidence points the same way: its intake ran through four Skills — three front
doors and a sibling refresher for one job — and Chapter 1's audit deferred all four here with the
collapse question logged rather than answered. This record answers it.

**What was given up, and it is real:** field input carries the findings discipline even for casual
reading — a capture costs a recorded line with a type, a state, and provenance, where a bookmark
would have cost nothing. The length bound is the mitigation: material matching a known class
records a reference and the delta, so the twentieth capture costs a line, not a page.

**What was considered and rejected:** a lightweight capture surface — an inbox short of the
findings registers. It is a second triage queue under a name, and a pass that must drain two
queues is a pass that drains the cheaper one. Also rejected: porting any of the four Skills at
ratification. Chapter 1 admits a Skill on receipts — a job actually done repeatedly — and this
repository has not yet run intake even once.
